## Save and bundle local copies on your Mac (recommended before publishing)
Run the provided `download-assets.sh` in a terminal **on your own Mac with internet access**. This downloads the published images to `assets/` and updates local paths in `index.html` only after downloads succeed. It does not touch GitHub. The official logo is placed on a white background in the dark footer to preserve its original colors.
