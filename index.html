ABOUT

About the survey

This survey invites women undergraduate and graduate students in Baskin Engineering to share their perspectives on academic opportunities, belonging, respectful learning environments, and student support.

The goal is to identify areas for improvement and inform future conversations and recommendations within Baskin Engineering.

Privacy & participation

Participation is voluntary. The Google Form is intended for UCSC account holders and is configured separately from this website. The questionnaire is designed not to request names or email addresses in responses.

Members of the project team may review individual responses, including written comments. Please avoid sharing names or other identifying information. Complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed, particularly in free-text responses.

Account restrictions and response collection settings should be verified in Google Forms before survey distribution.
