This survey is intended for women students at UC Santa Cruz. It invites perspectives on campus experiences related to diversity, equity, and inclusion.

01

Our purpose

To better understand student experiences and gather insights that may help inform future conversations and improvements.

02

Privacy & participation

Participation is voluntary. The survey is planned not to request names or email addresses. Sign-in, access, and one-response settings will be verified before launch.

Some responses could still be identifying when combined with other details; please share only what you are comfortable sharing.
